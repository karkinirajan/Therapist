# Intake & Baseline Process

Welcome to the **First-Session Intake** flow. This guide explains how to effectively complete and utilize the intake baseline snapshot, ensuring your personalized roadmap and tracking are properly configured.

## 📌 Purpose of Intake
The intake process runs **once** at the beginning of your journey (though it can be redone if necessary). Your answers here act as a foundation—a Baseline Snapshot. We use this snapshot to:
1. **Unlock check-ins** and daily tracking.
2. **Build your 6-month roadmap** dynamically tailored to your responses.
3. **Set a baseline** to measure future progress against.

---

## 📝 Sections Overview

### 1. Current Baseline
This section captures your immediate state. Be as honest and present as possible.
- **Mood, Anxiety & Energy:** Rate these on a 1-10 scale. Use your current feeling at this exact moment.
- **Sleep Quality:** Select the option that best describes your typical night's sleep over the past week.
- **Medication Adherence:** Reflect on the past 2 weeks. Be honest—no judgment here, it's just data.

### 2. The Three Areas That Feel Off-Track
We focus on concrete reality, not just vague feelings. For each area, provide **one specific, recent example** of how it felt off-track.
- **Career / Work:** E.g., "I froze during the presentation on Tuesday and avoided my manager for two days."
- **Daily Structure / Time:** E.g., "I spent 4 hours doomscrolling on Saturday instead of doing laundry."
- **General Life:** Encompasses relationships, health, or environment.

### 3. What's Already Working
Don't reinvent the wheel. If you have coping mechanisms, habits, or past treatment strategies that currently work for you, list them here. This ensures we build *upon* your strengths rather than replacing them.

### 4. Non-Negotiables
What has backfired for you in the past? 
- Did forced "toxic positivity" make you feel worse?
- Did rigid schedules lead to burnout? 
List your boundaries here so they can be respected in your roadmap.

### 5. Check-in Cadence
Choose how often you want to check in (e.g., daily, weekly). **Be realistic.** It is better to consistently check in weekly than to inconsistently attempt daily check-ins.

---

## ⚙️ After Submission
Once you hit **"Save baseline & build roadmap"**:
- Your profile gets updated with this new baseline.
- You will be redirected to the Dashboard.
- Your customized 6-month roadmap will be generated based on your specific pain points and non-negotiables.

> **Note:** If things drastically change or you feel your baseline is no longer accurate, you can return to the intake page and click **"Redo intake"** to recalibrate.
